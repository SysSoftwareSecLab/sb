# Manuscript figures

These are the exact four PNG assets referenced by `../main.tex` and embedded in
`../paper.pdf`. Their hashes, paper roles, evidence inputs, and claim boundaries
are recorded in `FIGURE_PROVENANCE.json`.

The portable revalidation script checks the printed numerical payload of
Figures 3 and 4 directly from the supplied rows. It also checks each image hash.
It does not claim deterministic regeneration of the artwork from a clean
environment. Figures 1 and 2 explain the benchmark and a representative
scenario; neither is hardware-safety evidence.
